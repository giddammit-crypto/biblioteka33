## 2024-05-22 - [Skip Links & Semantic Tag Closure]
**Learning:** Skip links are often forgotten in custom themes but are crucial for keyboard users. Also, splitting semantic tags like `<main>` across `header.php` and `footer.php` is common but requires careful verification to ensure they are properly closed.
**Action:** Always verify `header.php` for skip links and `footer.php` for matching closing tags of containers opened in the header.
